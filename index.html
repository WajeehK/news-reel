About

News Reel reads public RSS feeds from BBC, Guardian, Sky News, NPR, Al Jazeera, CBS and Google News (incl. Reuters & AP) every ~15 minutes. Live News: FinancialJuice headlines straight from its public feed every 60 seconds while open, Al Jazeera headlines every ~15 minutes, Al Jazeera English live stream via YouTube. Story images belong to their publishers and link to the original articles. Logos from Simple Icons (CC0) or the brand's own site icon; flags from flag-icons (MIT). Portraits from Wikimedia Commons via Wikipedia, used under their free licences: . Prices: CoinGecko, Binance (live Bitcoin) and Yahoo Finance (delayed). Economic calendar: Forex Factory weekly feed plus official Fed, BoE and ECB dates; actuals from FinancialJuice. Map: Natural Earth (public domain). Outlet lean ratings are approximate, loosely based on public AllSides / Ad Fontes style charts. Push alerts are relayed via ntfy.sh, end-to-end encrypted to this app.
