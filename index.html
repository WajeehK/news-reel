About

News Reel reads public RSS feeds from BBC, Guardian, Sky News, NPR, Al Jazeera, CBS and Google News (incl. Reuters & AP) every ~15 minutes. Story images belong to their publishers and link to the original articles. Logos from Simple Icons (CC0) or the brand's own site icon; flags from flag-icons (MIT).
